Download. Summarize. Share.

BabakCast lets you grab YouTube, X (Twitter), Instagram, and LinkedIn videos, copy tweet text, and turn transcripts into summaries using your AI provider. No backend, no accounts, no tracking.

Download APK View GitHub

What it does

YouTube, X, Instagram & LinkedIn download

Paste a YouTube, X (Twitter), Instagram, or LinkedIn URL, get shareable video or audio files. Keep audio whole or split it at 16 MB — split parts are tagged “Part n of N” so they play in order.

X/Twitter all-media download

Download all photos, videos, and GIFs from an X/Twitter post in one tap. Supports multi-image tweets, mixed media, and animated GIFs — share everything together.

Tweet text copy & share

Paste an X/Twitter URL and tap Copy Text to copy the tweet to your clipboard, or Share Text to open the share sheet — no media download needed.

Transcript to summary

Extract YouTube captions and summarize with your chosen AI model. Bullets, paragraph, or TL;DR.

Bring your own AI

OpenAI, Azure, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter. Set API key and model in Settings.

Stays on device

Everything runs locally. API keys stored with Android EncryptedSharedPreferences.

Persian translation on share

Optionally translate shared text to Persian with AI — original and translation go out together.

BabakCast is a personal-use tool. It does not ship with API keys, ads, analytics, or accounts. You control your data and your AI provider.
